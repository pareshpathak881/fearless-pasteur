## 1. Regional Overview

The Asia-Pacific region is experiencing an unprecedented capital expenditure cycle across water distribution and metering networks. Rapid urbanization, combined with aging municipal pipelines in Tier-1 and Tier-2 metropolitan areas, has prompted governments to prioritize non-revenue water (NRW) reduction.

## 2. Infrastructure Capex Sizing

Total municipal water infrastructure capital expenditure across APAC reached USD 54.2 billion in 2024. Of this total, digital metering and pipeline monitoring solutions accounted for an increasing proportion of public utility budgets.

### Key Metrics
- **2024 APAC Municipal Infrastructure Capex:** USD 54.2 Billion.
- **Smart Meter Penetration Rate in APAC Cities:** 22.0% in 2024, projected to expand to 58.0% by 2030.
- **Top Investing Nations:** China (46% of regional capex), India (21%), Japan (14%), and Australia (8%).
- **Scope Note:** Covers municipal civil engineering, pipeline rehabilitation, digital metering networks, and district pumping stations.
