## 1. Executive Summary

The global residential water purification equipment market is experiencing rapid expansion driven by heightened consumer awareness regarding microplastics, PFAS ("forever chemicals"), heavy metals, and microbiological contamination in municipal tap water.

Technological advancements in multi-stage reverse osmosis (RO), ultrafiltration (UF), and ultraviolet (UV) LED purification systems have democratized access to point-of-use (POU) and point-of-entry (POE) water treatment systems.

## 2. Market Sizing and Forecast (2024–2030)

The global residential water purifier market was valued at USD 9.45 billion in 2024. The sector is forecast to expand at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 8.4% from 2025 to 2030, reaching USD 15.32 billion by 2030.

### Sizing and Growth Highlights
- **2024 Global Market Size:** USD 9.45 Billion.
- **CAGR (2025–2030):** 8.4%.
- **Scope Note:** Covers Point-of-Use (countertop, under-sink, faucet mount) and Point-of-Entry residential systems, including replacement filter cartridge consumable revenue.
- **Regional Dominance:** Asia-Pacific accounts for 44.0% of worldwide revenue in 2024 (USD 4.16 billion).

## 3. Technology Segmentation

- **Reverse Osmosis (RO):** Represents 53.4% of total market revenue, dominating regions with high total dissolved solids (TDS) like India, China, and the American Southwest.
- **Ultrafiltration (UF):** Represents 24.1% of market value, favored in gravity-fed and low-energy applications.
- **UV Disinfection & Carbon Block:** Accounts for 22.5% of market value.
