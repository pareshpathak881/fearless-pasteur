## 1. Executive Summary

Industrial water reuse, semiconductor manufacturing pure-water requirements, and pharmaceutical ultrapure water mandates are driving massive investments into commercial and industrial (C&I) water filtration infrastructure.

## 2. Market Sizing & Projections

The global C&I water filtration systems market was valued at USD 6.15 billion in 2024. The industry is projected to grow at a CAGR of 7.1% from 2025 to 2030.

### Sizing Metrics
- **2024 Global Market Size:** USD 6.15 Billion.
- **Forecast CAGR (2025–2030):** 7.1%.
- **Scope Note:** Encompasses modular multi-media filters, industrial membrane bioreactors (MBR), electrodeionization (EDI), and commercial continuous filtration skids.
- **Regional Breakdown:** North America holds 34.0% share, followed closely by Asia-Pacific at 31.0% and Europe at 26.5%.

## 3. End-Use Verticals

Key industrial adopters include:
- Semiconductor and microelectronics fabrication (demanding sub-ppb ultrapure water).
- Food & beverage processing (sanitary membrane separation).
- Energy and power generation (boiler feed water demineralization).
- Municipal wastewater reclamation projects.
