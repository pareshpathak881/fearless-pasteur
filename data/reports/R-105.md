## 1. Scope and Executive Summary

This report covers wireless IoT sensor networks, lift-station telemetry nodes, chemical dosing automation, and biological oxygen demand (BOD) online analyzers deployed across municipal sewage and industrial wastewater treatment plants.

## 2. Market Sizing & Compound Growth

The global IoT wastewater monitoring market reached USD 2.95 billion in 2024. Driven by environmental discharge penalties and digital twin modeling, the market is poised to expand at a CAGR of 13.5% through 2030.

### Metric Overview
- **2024 Global Valuation:** USD 2.95 Billion.
- **CAGR (2024–2030):** 13.5%.
- **Regional Distribution:** Europe leads with 36.0% market share due to stringent Urban Wastewater Treatment Directives (UWWTD), followed by Asia-Pacific at 29.0% and North America at 28.0%.
- **Scope Note:** Hardware sensors (DO, pH, ORP, turbidity), gateway edge computers, and cloud SCADA software.
